INSTAGRAM CAMPAIGN · 10 OCT – 2 NOV 2026

What we do

One subject a day. A video is made, put on creator accounts, and carried the same day onto political pages and meme pages. The job is to set the narrative, and to answer the other side while people are still watching.

01

UGC videos

Creators post the video on their own accounts. We use every size, from under 20,000 followers to 500,000 and above, so the line is not stuck on one kind of page.

02

Three posting lanes

The same subject then moves on Insta Political, Insta Meme and Insta Meme Premium. Political says it straight. Meme makes it travel. Premium is where the larger views have come.

03

Same day, not the next day

Set our narrative. Counter theirs before the day moves on. The count and the views stay on the record, category by category.

CAMPAIGN REPORT01
10 OCT – 3 NOV · 01–03

Narratives we ran

10 October to 3 November, in the order they ran.

01

Elon Musk and the Starlink ‘oligarchs’ claim

Countered Elon Musk’s tweet claiming Indian ‘oligarchs’ are blocking Starlink, a ‘crime against the people of India’. Set it against Starlink’s own pricing abroad, a $1,500 ‘demand surcharge’ plus $130 a month shown by a US user, and noted India has no official Starlink price yet. Used the 4G-village figure, 11,256 of about 6.44 lakh villages, and the Communications Ministry clarification that all satcom licensees are at the same security-check stage.

02

CJP, Abhijit Dipke and the Delhi Police video

Exposed the Cockroach Janta Party narrative around Abhijit Dipke, detained with Ashutosh Ranka and Saurav Das before an unpermitted Jantar Mantar protest. CJP claimed police did not tell Dipke about his grandmother’s death on 10 October or let him call family. A Delhi Police video, presented by DCP Neha Yadav, shows police informing him and offering a call, a trip home and travel, which he refused. The trio’s release on a one-year peace bond was being billed a ‘legal victory’.

03

Congress out of power for decades

Called out Congress and the Gandhi-Vadra family for long gaps without Chief Ministers, against the pitch of ‘Indian democracy vs one family’s zid’ and of seeking power through the street rather than votes. The last Congress CM in UP was 1989, Gujarat 1995, Odisha 2000, Bengal 1977, Tamil Nadu 1967, and never in Jharkhand. Years out of power: Tamil Nadu about 59, Bengal 49, UP 37, Bihar 36, with reliance on DMK, RJD, JMM, NC and SP. Tripura was later corrected to 1993 and Andhra Pradesh to 2014.

Narratives we ran

04

INC Kerala’s ‘Dear Surya Kant, resign now’ tweet

Called out the tweet telling CJI Surya Kant to ‘resign now, we will find your replacement’, posted after he declined a closed-room meeting with Rahul Gandhi and the Congress president, citing matters that were sub judice. Set ‘Samvidhan bachao’ against a threat to the CJI, with the line ‘tab Emergency thi, aaj tweet hai’. The party deleted the tweet on 12 October.

05

Amit Shah vs Rahul Gandhi on EVMs and ‘vote chori’

Used Amit Shah’s rebuttal. EVMs came in 2004, but Congress has lost Gujarat since 1990, won only 14 of 4,600 seats in ballot-paper polls in Tripura, and has kept losing since Rahul became its leader. The rally of about 8,000 over 1.5 km was mocked as ‘desh ki aawaz’. The infiltrator and CAA angle was added.

06

Prashant Bhushan’s ‘regime change’ and CJP

Called out CJP’s link to the ‘regime change’ remark Prashant Bhushan made on stage, with Prakash Raj shown present and a Bangladesh student-protest comparison. Set his 2011 call for a Kashmir plebiscite against the 2026 remark: two videos, 15 years apart, same soch. Rahul’s vote-chori and EVM claims were tied to CJP’s ‘no politics’ Gen-Z face. Dipke and Neha Bora were kept out of the conference line, since they do not appear in the clip.

07

Supreme Court notice on the SIR petition

On 15 October a bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi issued notice to the Election Commission, CEC Gyanesh Kumar and the other Election Commissioners on the SIR petition by Rahul Gandhi and nine other INDIA bloc leaders. The bench said one of the petitioners would have to explain his conduct. The order named Sanjay Raut. The CJI had earlier declined the opposition leaders’ request for an appointment.

08

Rajnath Singh’s press conference

Carried his 16 October points. Rahul should respect the janmat. The vote-chori claim has no proof. The ‘hydrogen bomb’ was not even a pataakha. Elections ran for five decades without voter cards. Congress was called out for making the EC a ‘kathputli’, for the Emergency, and for using youth as ‘mohra’.

09

CJP ‘Lehra Do Tiranga’ march flop: Delhi and Mumbai

Permission was denied for the Connaught Place march during Navratri. Dipke was detained for the second time that week. Neha Bohra was detained on the way. CP shops stayed empty. The tiranga was being used as a shield for people who had said ‘regime change’. The 17 October Mumbai protest at Azad Maidan, held for jailed co-founders Ranka and Das, drew barely 50–150 people, per a TOI clipping.

10

France riots versus CJP’s Delhi march

Set the 17 October France riots against the Connaught Place march Delhi Police did not permit. Cars burnt in Paris, a Lille Apple store emptied, the Limoges town hall roof burnt, 1,200-plus arrests, 150 police hurt. Delhi Police stopped a crowd with a stated ‘regime change’ agenda during Navratri. Global liberal media called the French protesters rioters and the Indian ones ‘youth voice’.

11

Rahul Gandhi’s Vietnam trip

He left Delhi for Hanoi on Sunday 18 October, reportedly for a four-day break, with Congress silent while its SIR protests continued. He had been to Vietnam in January 2026, vanished for about two months in 2015, and did only a 2 km padyatra in Goa after the 12 October ‘March for Democracy’. He had just ‘guaranteed’ to deal with Modi, Amit Shah and Gyanesh Kumar. Indian Express called it a festive-week pause, and it sat before the rally.

12

Sanjay Raut’s Albania remark

The run of attacks went from the Election Commission, vote chori and a demand for CEC Gyanesh Kumar’s resignation, to the Supreme Court, and it was linked to Rahul Gandhi’s trip abroad. Raut got a Supreme Court show-cause notice on 16 October over a remark about the CJI, then posted that Albania’s people burnt the Supreme Court and all countries should learn, especially India. BJP’s Kirit Somaiya’s list was quoted, with the question of whether foreign-based planning sits behind it.

13

Rahul Gandhi and the ‘enemies of India’

Used the PM’s Dussehra call to recognise the nation’s enemies, and asked who applauds Rahul Gandhi. Pakistan’s former minister Fawad Chaudhry shared his video in 2024. Khalistani terrorist Pannun backed his September 2024 US remarks on Sikhs. A China chapter, and statements made on foreign soil.

14

Why Congress chose 1 November

The ‘Vote Bachao, Desh Bachao’ rally was fixed on the anniversary of 1 November 1984. About 2,700 Delhi deaths in official reports, at Trilokpuri, Sultanpuri, Mangolpuri and Palam. Rajiv Gandhi’s ‘bada ped girta hai’ line. Sajjan Kumar’s 2018 life sentence. Rahul’s 2018 London remark. Tilak Vihar Widow Colony and Jagdish Kaur’s Raj Nagar case. Chandni Chowk traders’ Rs 10,000 crore loss claim. The close was ‘365 din mein yahi din mila?’.

15

CJP leaders in Delhi High Court

Justice Subramonium Prasad asked Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka their age. The ‘Gen Z’ branding turned out to mean about 31-year-olds. After the petition was withdrawn, a Special Executive Magistrate released them on written undertakings to keep the peace and not protest at Jantar Mantar until its legal status changes. The ‘Truth has triumphed’ claim was called out.

The ‘regime change’ network

Used Fox News Digital’s Asra Nomani investigation, ‘Global Chaos Inc Grows to 101 Groups’, reported by the Times of India, on a network amplifying protests in Europe: BreakThrough News, CODEPINK, PSL. CODEPINK’s Jodie Evans asked on 14 October, ‘Where are we? Why are we not in the streets?’ about France, Spain and India. Linked to CJP, the 10 Janpath photo and the 1 November rally.

Rahul Gandhi, ‘failed product’

A product-recall metaphor. Lok Sabha seats of 44 in 2014, 52 in 2019 and 99 in 2024. Version history. ‘Brand ambassador changed, product same.’ ‘Customer care always on holiday.’ 99-plus election losses, the Vietnam trip during the SIR protests, and a family-owned product. Based partly on a Hindi ‘failed product’ article.

Jamia ‘Free Sharjeel Imam’ slogans

‘Free Kashmir’ and ‘Free Sharjeel Imam’ appeared on Jamia Millia Islamia walls on 22 October, and CCTV cables were cut. On 23 October Congress MP Imran Masood and SP MP Mohibbullah Nadvi reached the campus, were detained by Delhi Police and later released. The question was whether they sought the arrest of the slogan-writers or gave them a platform. A JNU 2016 comparison.

Justice Sanjay Kishan Kaul’s book and Congress

It dragged in the PM’s late father at a tribute event. Earlier lines were listed: 2014 ‘chaiwala’, 2017 ‘neech’, 2022 ‘Ravan’, 2023 ‘poisonous snake’. Linked to Rahul being abroad and to the 1 November rally.

21

Congress’s ‘imported youth faces’

The SIR agitation was led by CJP’s Dipke, Neha Bora, Saurav Das and Ashutosh Ranka, not by Congress’s own Youth Congress and NSUI. The question was who fills the stage at the 1 November rally if the movement’s faces are outsiders. Assam CM Himanta Sarma, once a Congress leader, was the contrast.

22

Rahul Gandhi and Congress in humour formats

Diary, a missing-person search, a report card, an excuse generator, the ‘Kaun Banega Neta’ quiz, a station-announcement spoof, a Congress weather bulletin and a job advertisement. Rahul was seen at Delhi airport on 18 October heading for Vietnam, was absent from the Patna event on 25 October, with the 1 November rally ahead. Included the ‘hydrogen bomb that never exploded’ line.

23

The Election Commission affidavit versus ‘vote chori’

The 26 October press note: the counter-affidavit was approved unanimously by all three commissioners, and the three SIR orders of 24 June 2025, 27 October 2025 and 14 May 2026 were also unanimous. The 28 July order never took effect. Form 6 was not changed, only the Centre can alter it, and from 21 October 2026 Form 6 and the SIR declaration appear separately. The rift is in Congress’s story, not the Commission.

24

Kerala Congress apologises to the CJI

The Kerala unit tendered an unconditional apology to CJI Surya Kant, in reply to a legal notice from SCBA joint secretary Meenesh Dubey over the 12 October post. KPCC president Sunny Joseph said the post was published by Vijay Thottathil without leadership approval, that he had been removed and asked to apologise, and that the demand was inappropriate. The question left open was when Rahul Gandhi apologises for the push against the CJI and the Supreme Court.

25

Matrize survey: 68% on the Patna remark

A phone survey by Matrize News Communications, which PTI calls a little-known agency, on 25–27 October with 5,000 respondents. 68% felt the Congress president’s remark undermined the dignity of the PM’s office. 54% saw desperation. 72% saw disrespect to Sanatan culture and Indian values. Used as the public reaction to the Patna line.

26

Madhya Pradesh’s AI procurement monitoring

Congress’s video claimed Chief Minister Mohan Yadav’s government was exposed. The full clip shows ACS Sanjay Dubey saying the AI system compares every municipal purchase from the GeM and e-Nagar Palika portals. It caught phenyl bought at Rs 33,500 and an LED bulb at Rs 11,800. At training time 146 officers faced action. The figure is now 392 officers, including 135 CMOs, with 7 dismissals and Rs 3–4 crore in recoveries.

27

UP cabinet clears four link expressways

On 27 October the cabinet approved four link expressways, Farrukhabad, Jewar, Jhansi, and the Agra-Lucknow to Purvanchal link, estimated at Rs 17,542 crore with a three-year target. Set the Yogi government’s road-building against Congress’s rally and street protest.

28

Karnataka CM: ‘no money’ for sugarcane farmers

On 26 October hundreds of sugarcane farmers blocked the highway at Belagavi’s Suvarna Vidhana Soudha, demanding at least Rs 5,500 a tonne, with Rs 1,000 from the state. The CM said the government can only spend what is in its account. Tied to the 2023 five guarantees, the Nandini milk hike, and a treasury-empty charge.

29

‘Cash for ticket’ outside 10 Janpath

Gaurav Kumar sat outside 10 Janpath on 28 October, alleging he paid Rs 7 crore for a ticket for his wife Suchitra Devi, a former Haryana Mahila Congress general secretary, with money allegedly reaching Priyanka Gandhi Vadra. NDTV reported a Delhi Police FIR and his WhatsApp, bank and audio evidence. BJP’s Pradeep Bhandari had raised it in March 2026. The Gandhi family has not responded.

30

‘Pappu Media’ and NewsClick

Outlets echoing Rahul Gandhi, and the question of who funds them. The ED appeal in the Supreme Court on NewsClick: about Rs 86 crore of foreign money, of which Rs 9.59 crore as foreign investment and about Rs 76 crore from a US Justice and Education Fund as ‘media services’. An August 2017 email about taking foreign investment without ministry permission.

31

MLC results: ballot paper, Congress zero, Varanasi

After the 23 October ballot-paper polls for 28 MLC seats in Karnataka, Bihar, UP and Maharashtra, Congress had not opened its account, including 0 of 4 in Karnataka and Madan Mohan Jha’s 324-vote loss in Bihar. The question posed was, if the EVM is the thief, who is the thief on ballot paper? On Varanasi, Samajwadi Party’s Lal Bihari Yadav held his own seat and Congress won none of UP’s 11 MLC seats. Jairam Ramesh’s comment was turned back on him.

32

Satish Jarkiholi on SIR

Karnataka’s own Congress minister said on camera there is no issue with SIR in Karnataka, and that the deleted names are dead, shifted or duplicated voters. The 27 October final list fell from 5.54 crore to about 4.55 crore. The question was why Rahul does not hold a press conference now. Congress states were set against BJP states on ‘vote chori’.

33

‘People’s Impeachment’ of the CEC

The INDIA bloc’s plan to ‘impeach’ CEC Gyanesh Kumar at the Congress president’s residence at 3 pm. First called Mock Parliament, then renamed People’s Impeachment after worries it would be seen as disrespect to Parliament, per NDTV. The question was why the Leader of the Opposition needs a nakli Parliament when he has the real one. Turnout claims grew by the day.

34

Flavio Bolsonaro and ‘vote chori’

He lost the 25 October runoff to Lula, then from Miami claimed he was the elected president and the election was stolen, before deleting the post. His own party’s Senate leader said there is no evidence. Brazil’s election court and observers accepted the result. The parallel was Rahul Gandhi’s ‘vote chori’ claim after each defeat.

35

Nandini milk price hike

Congress’s 2023 ‘Save Nandini’ campaign, then milk from Rs 39 a litre in May 2023 to Rs 54 from 1 November, a rise of about 40 per cent in about 40 months. Moneycontrol reported the Cabinet decided on 6 October but held the announcement until the Legislative Council polls, where Congress still lost all 4 seats. The Rs 8 jump hits the household kitchen.

36

World Bank poverty report

About 49.6 crore people left poverty in the past decade and extreme poverty fell to about 2.6 per cent. Set against Indira’s 1971 ‘Garibi hatao’ and the 2004 ‘Congress ka haath, aam aadmi ke saath’. The line used was ‘naara Congress ka, kaam Modi ji ka’, with free ration for 80 crore, Ujjwala, Jan Dhan, PM Awas and Har Ghar Jal.

37

Polymarket ‘Modi out’

On the market ‘Modi out by 31 December 2026?’, 96–97 per cent of traders said no. Polymarket was hacked on 29 October via a third-party service, with about $2.6 million, around Rs 22 crore, taken from user wallets, and a CFTC probe was reported. A betting site does not decide the term. 140 crore voters do. The next Lok Sabha election is in 2029.

38

‘Modi kahin nahi jayega’

Chants at the PM’s roadshow and farmers’ rally in Banaskantha on 30 October, women holding posters, reported by PTI, India TV and Moneycontrol. Tied to a developed, self-reliant India. The public’s answer to the Patna remark.

39

Diljit Dosanjh and the woman CRPF officer

At JLN Stadium on 30 October, without naming the Cockroach Janta Party, he said he is an artist and not to teach Punjabis how to fight, and defended Satluj, on Jaswant Singh Khalra, against being mocked. He paused the show to salute Delhi Police and CRPF, and hugged a woman CRPF officer on stage, saying she reminded him of his mother. CJP’s Abhijeet Dipke attended and posted a conciliatory open letter on 31 October, per PTI.

40

Diwali traders versus the 1 November rally

CTI, led by Brijesh Goyal, asked Rahul Gandhi to move the rally to after Bhai Dooj, 10 November, with letters also to Sonia Gandhi, Akhilesh Yadav and Mamata Banerjee. CTI says about Rs 25,000 crore of Dhanteras-to-Diwali trade is at stake, with about Rs 10,000 crore hit by closures in Chandni Chowk, Sadar Bazar and Karol Bagh. Traders did not oppose protest. The loss falls on diya-makers, rangoli sellers and hawkers.

41

Ramlila Maidan: 10 lakh versus the ground

Drone footage showed half the ground empty. Delhi Police said the ground holds only 30–40 thousand with chairs. Sambit Patra said 5–7 thousand of the 28,000 chairs were empty. Congress leaders had claimed 10 lakh. Also used were the 44, 52, 99 seat arc and the Diwali market loss.

42

The 13.5 crore ‘vote chori’ calculation

At the rally he said 13.5 crore voters, 15 per cent, were removed after the 2024 election, the NDA’s margin was 3 per cent, so 2024 was an illegal election. On 2 November Sambit Patra said SIR is verification, not final deletion, and that names flagged as dead, shifted or duplicate can be restored through claims, objections and Form 6. The earlier breakup was about 6 crore shifted, 3 crore dead, 1 crore duplicate and 3 crore untraceable or alleged infiltrators.

43

CRPF: a year with no operational fatality

Twelve consecutive months without an operational fatality, announced by Director General G. P. Singh on 1 November. The last was on 30 October 2025. The first such stretch since the one that ended in January 1979. He credited better hardware, training and planning, and faster evacuation of the injured.

44

PM Modi at the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit

On 2 November: India will add 500 GW of non-fossil capacity over the next 10–11 years, with more than Rs 20 lakh crore of renewable investment opportunity by 2030. Rooftop solar for cooking and EV charging, solar on public buildings and farms, green hydrogen and indigenous batteries.

45

Rahul Gandhi abroad again

On 1 November Sambit Patra mocked the ‘shop in Chandni Chowk’ appeal by pointing to the Vietnam and Thailand stamps in the passport. On 3 November Pradeep Bhandari posted about a planned US visit to New York and Chicago on 16–19 November: no vote chori, only kaam chori. BJP also cites 54 foreign trips, estimated at about Rs 60 crore, per Patra.
