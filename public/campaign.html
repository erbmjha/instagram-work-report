What we do

01

02

03

Same day, not the next day

CAMPAIGN REPORT01
28 OCT – 3 NOV · ATTACK ON CONGRESS

Narratives we ran

Twenty lines. The heading is the line. The paragraph is what it rests on.

01

Ballot-paper MLC polls: Congress draws a blank. SP’s Varanasi wins are only status quo

In 28 teachers’ and graduates’ seats counted on 27–29 October, the BJP-led NDA won 16. Congress was shut out in UP, Bihar and Karnataka, including all four seats in Karnataka, where it is in government. The PM took the result up on X. In Varanasi, SP held both the Teachers’ seat and the Graduates’ seat. Those are retentions, not new losses. Congress did win the Nagpur Graduates’ seat, so the blank is in those three states, not in every one of the 28.

02

Kerala Congress apologises to the CJI. When will Rahul Gandhi apologise?

On 12 October the Kerala Congress handle told CJI Surya Kant to resign, after he declined to meet the Rahul Gandhi and Kharge delegation on SIR. After a legal notice, KPCC president Sunny Joseph apologised unconditionally. The post was deleted and Vijay Thottathil was removed. Rahul Gandhi led that march to the Supreme Court. No apology from him has been reported.

03

68% say Kharge’s remark lowered the PM’s office. 72% see disrespect to Sanatan

Kharge said “Modi ka baap bhi jayega” in Patna on 25 October. In a Matrize phone survey of 5,000 people, taken over the next two days, 68% said it undermined the dignity of the PM’s office and 72% said it showed a lack of respect for Sanatan culture. The figures are that agency’s findings.

04

“Modi kahin nahi jayega.” Gujarat answers Kharge

On 30 October, through the PM’s Banaskantha visit, crowds chanted “Modi kahin nahi jayega”. The BJP put a song out on the slogan. PTI and the Gujarat press reported the chants.

05

Karnataka minister Jarkiholi backs SIR, against Rahul Gandhi’s line

On 28–29 October Satish Jarkiholi said SIR in Karnataka was done in the right manner, and defended the deletion of nearly one crore names. The roll fell from 5.54 crore to 4.55 crore. That cut against the “vote chori” line. On 30 October he said the remarks were misinterpreted and that he stands with Rahul Gandhi.

06

“If money is not available, how can the govt pay?”

On 26 October at Belagavi, sugarcane farmers asked for Rs 5,500 a tonne and a Rs 1,000 state top-up. CM D.K. Shivakumar told them: “If money is not available, how can the govt pay? This is not my personal money.”

07

Karnataka raises milk by Rs 8 a litre, the day the MLC code ends

On 29 October the Cabinet cleared the hike from 1 November. Toned milk goes from Rs 46 to Rs 54. Indian Express called it the steepest rise in the state’s history. It was held back until the MLC code ended, and it landed on households just before Deepavali.

08

Rs 7 crore ticket allegation, outside 10 Janpath

On 28 October Gaurav Kumar protested outside 10 Janpath, threatened self-immolation, and demanded Rs 7 crore back, saying it was paid for a Congress ticket. He named Priyanka Gandhi Vadra. Most reports place the ticket at the Bawal assembly seat. The allegation is unproven.

09

Delhi traders asked for the rally to move. The letters were ignored

CTI chairman Brijesh Goyal wrote to Rahul Gandhi three times, then on 30 October to Sonia Gandhi, Akhilesh Yadav and Mamata Banerjee, asking that the 1 November rally wait till after Diwali or shift to Burari. CTI put the loss at Rs 10,000–12,000 crore and said about 3 lakh shops were hit. Those figures are CTI’s estimate.

10

Ramlila Maidan: the rally did not fill the ground

Congress projected a crowd of 10 lakh. Sambit Patra said about 40% of the ground was empty, and video showed people leaving while Rahul spoke. Turnout is disputed. The answer put out was “Loktantra Bachao, Arajakta Hatao”.

11

The rally was held on the 1984 anniversary

1 November is tied to the anti-Sikh violence. Sambit Patra said survivors had asked for the rally to move by a day or two, and that Rahul Gandhi refused. He recalled Rajiv Gandhi’s line, “when a big tree falls, the earth shakes”. The appeal is Patra’s account. Manmohan Singh apologised in Parliament in 2005. The point is the date chosen, and the Gandhi family’s conduct, not a claim that no apology was ever made.

12

Sanjay Raut: “Hum regime change ki taraf jaa rahe hain”

He told ANI a revolution is coming, that “we are moving towards regime change”, and that supporters would break barricades. That was put out as the agenda behind the “vote theft” campaign. Whether Congress owned the words is not established.

13

A mock Parliament, and a “public impeachment” of the CEC

On 31 October, at Kharge’s residence, the bloc staged an impeachment of CEC Gyanesh Kumar. Meira Kumar played Speaker. Rahul Gandhi called Kumar the first target. It has no legal effect. Amit Shah’s line was that Rahul can become PM only in a mock Parliament.

14

Amit Shah offers a debate in Parliament. The format is not taken

On 2 November in Hyderabad he said he was ready for a special session, on EVMs, SIR and infiltrators. Rahul Gandhi answered with a dare of his own, an open debate on any stage. He did not take the Parliament session.

15

If the 2024 election was illegal, so is the LoP post

At the rally, Rahul Gandhi called the 2024 election illegal. The answer: then his post as Leader of the Opposition, and every Congress MP’s mandate, would be illegal too. Congress has filed no election petition. An election is not declared illegal two and a half years later from a maidan. Gyanesh Kumar was an Election Commissioner in 2024. He became Chief Election Commissioner in February 2025.

16

Flavio Bolsonaro’s script is the same as “vote chori”

He lost the 25 October runoff in Brazil, alleged fraud without evidence, called himself the true president, and left for the US. Reject the result, allege fraud without proof, take the fight abroad. A government in exile has not been formed. His own party accepted Lula’s win.

17

Diljit Dosanjh answers the Cockroach brigade, and salutes a woman in uniform

On 30 October at JLN Stadium he told them not to teach Punjabis how to fight, after they mocked Satluj. He brought a woman CRPF officer on stage, saluted her, and thanked Delhi Police, CRPF and the Metro.

18

The sting: talk of a Nepal- or Bangladesh-style upheaval

On 2 November Times Now Navbharat aired an undercover recording. A Delhi University student leader and a Jamiat figure spoke of that kind of unrest in India. It was tied to the “regime change” line and to the PM’s warning on anarchy. These are the channel’s recordings. There is no independent confirmation.

19

“Priests commit rapes inside temples”

Neha Bora of AISA said it, citing the Kathua case, while backing the move against the CEC. On 3 November Pradeep Bhandari called it the same anti-Hindu line as earlier remarks on women near temples. She is not a Congress office-bearer, and has said she will not join. The link drawn to Congress is the BJP’s framing.

20

The Supreme Court asks Rahul Gandhi and Kharge to reply

On 3 November a bench headed by CJI Surya Kant sought their replies on the 12 October march to the court. Lawyers say a journalist, three months pregnant, was pushed and hospitalised. These are allegations, not a finding. The next date is 23 November.

CAMPAIGN REPORT02
28 OCT – 3 NOV · GOVERNMENT

What was delivered

Five decisions and one speech, from the same days.

22

Rs 1,856.67 crore for sports goods, under Khelo India

On 28 October the Cabinet cleared the scheme through 2033–34. Manufacturing zones, turnover-linked support, certification, skilling and export promotion. The point is less import, more jobs, and a sports-goods base of our own.

23

Rs 10,000 crore to make containers in India

Cleared the same day, running to 2039–40. A 3% interest subvention, support on operating cost, and money for testing. The target is a ten-fold rise in capacity, to 7.5 lakh TEUs, with about 3,000 direct jobs and over 50,000 indirect.

24

UP clears Rs 17,542 crore for four link expressways

On 27 October the Cabinet under CM Yogi Adityanath cleared civil works for the Farrukhabad, Jewar and Jhansi links, and the link between the Agra–Lucknow and Purvanchal expressways. Three years to build. They join five expressways already running.

25

49.6 crore people lifted above the poverty line

The World Bank’s October brief: at $4.20 a day, poverty fell from 57.7% to 16.1% between 2011 and 2023. Extreme poverty, at $3 a day, fell from 27.1% to 2.6%. The period includes the last UPA years. The line used is India’s reduction over that decade-plus, not a claim that one government did all of it.

26

Naxalism eliminated. The warning is now the “dimagi Naxal”

On 31 October at Ekta Nagar, Rashtriya Ekta Diwas, the PM said last year’s promise to free the country of Naxalism had been met. He warned that “dimagi Naxals” are trying to create unrest, and called the opposition an obstructive force. He asked who is meeting whom abroad, and who is speaking the language of India’s enemies.
