INSTAGRAM CAMPAIGN · 10 OCT – 2 NOV 2026

What we do

One subject a day. A video is made, put on creator accounts, and carried the same day onto political pages and meme pages. The job is to set the narrative, and to answer the other side while people are still watching.

01

UGC videos

Creators post the video on their own accounts. We use every size, from under 20,000 followers to 500,000 and above, so the line is not stuck on one kind of page.

02

Three posting lanes

The same subject then moves on Insta Political, Insta Meme and Insta Meme Premium. Political says it straight. Meme makes it travel. Premium is where the larger views have come.

03

Same day, not the next day

Set our narrative. Counter theirs before the day moves on. The count and the views stay on the record, category by category.

CAMPAIGN REPORT01
WHAT THE UGC VIDEOS CARRIED

Narratives we ran

Five subjects, in the order the UGC went out.

01

CJP’s protest was not a people’s protest

They put it on the street as a public cause. From 10 October it went out as their own mobilisation, so their picture of the street did not stay the picture of the day.

02

CJP went to court and did not get what they came for

They came for a result and left without it. It was a loss, not a hearing they simply sat through, and it was kept separate from the street protest.

03

1984 was put back in the open

Sikhs were killed. The state of that time let it happen. It was not left as a closed date, and this set of videos stood apart from CJP.

04

Amit Shah put Rahul Gandhi on the record

What he said was carried as he said it, not turned into a loose attack. It was number 1 under Politics on X, under #ShahExposesRahul.

05

The Ramlila Maidan rally failed

The claim and the ground were not the same. It was not the crowd it was said to be, and it was not a show of strength. One premium post crossed 1 million views, one crossed 300,000 reach, and two crossed 250,000.

Narratives we countered

01

02

03
